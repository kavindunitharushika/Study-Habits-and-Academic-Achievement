# The Relationship Between Study Habits and Academic Achievement of A/L Students

## Research Overview
This research project investigates the relationship between study habits and academic achievement among G.C.E. Advanced Level students in the Embilipitiya area in Sri Lanka.

## Research Objectives
- Examine the relationship between time management and academic achievement.
- Investigate the relationship between study techniques and academic achievement.
- Explore the relationship between study environment and academic achievement.
- Examine the relationship between motivation and academic achievement.

## Research Methodology
- **Research Approach:** Quantitative Research
- **Sample Size:** 100 A/L Students
- **Data Collection:** Structured Questionnaire
- **Statistical Software:** IBM SPSS Statistics
- **Statistical Methods:** Descriptive Statistics, Correlation Analysis, and Multiple Regression
